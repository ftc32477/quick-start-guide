### FTC 32477 Origin Quick Start Guide · August 2026, 1st Edition

This release publishes the offline (PDF) edition of the **FTC 32477 Origin Quick Start Guide, August 2026, 1st Edition**, in four languages:

- 简体中文 (zh-Hans)
- 繁體中文 (zh-Hant)
- English (US)
- Français

The guide contains 7 chapters: Preface, Team Essentials, Modeling & Design, Hardware & Build, Programming, Outreach & PR, and Afterword. It helps new members get started quickly and serves as a working baseline for returning members. Compiled by the editorial team of FTC Team 32477 Origin, Beijing National Day Experimental School. This guide is an independent publication of the team and is not affiliated with FIRST®.

For the online edition, visit: <https://ftc32477.github.io/docs/index.html>
