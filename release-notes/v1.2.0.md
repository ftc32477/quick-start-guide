### FTC 32477 Origin Quick Start Guide · August 2026, 3rd Edition

This release publishes the offline (PDF) edition of the **FTC 32477 Origin Quick Start Guide, August 2026, 3rd Edition**, in six languages:

- 简体中文 (zh-Hans)
- 繁體中文 (zh-Hant)
- English (US)
- Français
- Español
- 한국어 (ko)

New in this release:

- Added the complete Korean (한국어) edition: all seven chapters and the website pages (language homepage, version history) are now available in Korean, with a Korean PDF included.
- Standardized name annotations across all language editions: real person names now carry their original Chinese characters (e.g., Fu Xiuqi (付修齐); in Korean, names are given in their hanja readings, e.g., 부수제(付修齐)), and proper nouns such as platforms carry their source-language names (e.g., KIRIN (麒麟), Bilibili (哔哩哔哩)).

The guide contains 7 chapters: Preface, Team Essentials, Modeling & Design, Hardware & Build, Programming, Outreach & PR, and Afterword. It helps new members get started quickly and serves as a working baseline for returning members. Compiled by the editorial team of FTC Team 32477 Origin, Beijing National Day Experimental School. This guide is an independent publication of the team and is not affiliated with FIRST®.

For the online edition, visit: <https://ftc32477.github.io/docs/index.html>
