### FTC 32477 Origin Quick Start Guide · August 2026, 2nd Edition

This release publishes the offline (PDF) edition of the **FTC 32477 Origin Quick Start Guide, August 2026, 2nd Edition**, in five languages:

- 简体中文 (zh-Hans)
- 繁體中文 (zh-Hant)
- English (US)
- Français
- Español

New in this release:

- Added the complete Spanish (Español) edition: all seven chapters and the website pages (language homepage, version history) are now available in Spanish, with a Spanish PDF included.
- Updated the portal language card accent colors: #a61615 for Simplified/Traditional Chinese, #d85d23 for English (US)/Français, and #ffb953 for the new Spanish card.

The guide contains 7 chapters: Preface, Team Essentials, Modeling & Design, Hardware & Build, Programming, Outreach & PR, and Afterword. It helps new members get started quickly and serves as a working baseline for returning members. Compiled by the editorial team of FTC Team 32477 Origin, Beijing National Day Experimental School. This guide is an independent publication of the team and is not affiliated with FIRST®.
