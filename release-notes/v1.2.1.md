### FTC 32477 Origin Quick Start Guide · August 2026, 3rd Edition, 1st Revision

This release publishes the offline (PDF) edition of the **FTC 32477 Origin Quick Start Guide, August 2026, 3rd Edition, 1st Revision**, in six languages:

- 简体中文 (zh-Hans)
- 繁體中文 (zh-Hant)
- English (US)
- Français
- Español
- 한국어 (ko)

Changes in this revision:

- Traditional Chinese edition: person names now carry their Simplified Chinese originals (e.g., 付修齊（付修齐）); names identical across both scripts are not annotated.
- Simplified and Traditional Chinese editions: translated terms of foreign origin now carry their source-language names (e.g., 结构体（Part Studio）, 构建（Build）); the annotation convention for all languages is documented in the project README.
- Portal: the Korean language card accent color now matches the Spanish card (#ffb953).

The guide contains 7 chapters: Preface, Team Essentials, Modeling & Design, Hardware & Build, Programming, Outreach & PR, and Afterword. It helps new members get started quickly and serves as a working baseline for returning members. Compiled by the editorial team of FTC Team 32477 Origin, Beijing National Day Experimental School. This guide is an independent publication of the team and is not affiliated with FIRST®.

For the online edition, visit: <https://ftc32477.github.io/docs/index.html>
